The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) is experiencing increasing demand pressures, particularly in regional areas where service availability is constrained by thin provider markets. Analysis of participant data indicates that South Australia has a higher proportion of its population enrolled in the NDIS (approximately 3.3%) compared with larger states such as Victoria (approximately 2.9%), despite a substantially smaller population base. This elevates the risk of disproportionate funding pressure in regional South Australia.This project applies descriptive and predictive analytics, including linear, ridge, and lasso regression, to forecast NDIS funding growth in regional South Australia and compare it with regional Victoria. Model results indicate a sustained positive funding growth trend in regional South Australia, exceeding population growth patterns. Based on these findings, the study recommends proactive NDIA market-stewardship measures, including targeted provider incentives and workforce development, to ensure forecast funding growth translates into equitable service access rather than increased under-utilisation.
